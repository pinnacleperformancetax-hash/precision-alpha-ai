Privacy Policy

Precision Alpha AI • Last updated October 8, 2026

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider and we do not see or store it in readable form.
  • Brokerage connection: if you connect Alpaca, an authorization token that lets the service act on your connected account, plus account information we read from Alpaca such as balances, positions, orders and activity.
  • Your settings and activity: risk rules you choose, and the trades, logs and summaries the service produces for your account.
  • Basic technical data: information servers normally record, such as IP address and request times, used for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

To run the service for you: sign you in, show your dashboard, place and track orders you authorize, send alert and summary emails, keep the service secure, and fix problems. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who helps us run it

We use service providers that process data on our behalf: Supabase (sign-in and database), Render (backend hosting), Netlify (website hosting), Alpaca (brokerage), EmailJS and Gmail (alert emails), and Anthropic (AI analysis of market data and news; we do not send your name or email for this). Each handles data under its own terms and privacy policy. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Security

We use encrypted connections and limit access to the data. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account is open. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by emailing us. You can also remove our access to your Alpaca account at any time from your Alpaca account settings.

Children

The service is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes.
